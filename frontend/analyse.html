About your data

Selected passages of your text are sent to OpenAI to create the card. There are no accounts, saved repos or public posts. Your text and card are held temporarily in server memory, normally for up to 30 minutes after completion, and are lost on restart. Operational rate-limit counters, including keyed network and browser identifiers, are stored separately and cleared during later activity once expired. No announcement text is written to that database. Hosting and AI providers have their own retention policies. Paste published announcements, not confidential information.
