# Smallcaps.ai — Facts. No fluff. Output Contract v1

This contract defines the next public output for the Smallcaps.ai analyst engine.

It does not weaken any existing evidence, provenance, arithmetic, point-in-time, validation or impact-calibration rules. Where this contract is tighter on public wording, brevity or unsupported inference, this contract wins.

## Public positioning

**smallcaps.ai**  
**AIM company news. Facts. No fluff.**

The public experience is company news, not an RNS summariser. `RNS` may remain an internal source/ingestion term.

## Current AnalystNote field mapping

The engine still returns the richer internal `AnalystNote`. Do not discard analytical depth. Populate the existing fields so they project deterministically into the compact Company News product:

- public `direction` ← `impact_colour` (`green`/`amber`/`red`/`grey`);
- public `materiality` ← `impact_score` (1–5);
- public `news_type` ← `rns_type`;
- public `headline` ← `headline`;
- public `take` ← `takeaway`;
- public `material_facts` ← **all decision-useful** `key_facts`;
- public `changes` ← supported comparator-rich `key_facts`, `guidance_events`, and `what_changed` only where a valid prior state exists;
- public `baseline_status` ← `what_changed.coverage_status`;
- public `watch_next` ← `watch_items`;
- source provenance ← `source_references`, current `source_id`, and comparator source IDs.

`analyst_view`, impact drivers, management claims, disclosure assessment and deeper structured fields remain available internally for QA and deeper research. They are not an excuse to make the public `takeaway` verbose.

## Core output

For every announcement produce a compact public record with:

1. `direction` — positive / mixed / negative / neutral;
2. `materiality` — 1–5, independent of direction;
3. `news_type` — canonical company-news category;
4. `headline` — concise economic outcome;
5. `take` — short investor shorthand;
6. `material_facts` — all decision-useful disclosed facts;
7. `changes` — supported deltas only;
8. `baseline_status` — building / established;
9. `watch_next` — optional measurable next evidence;
10. source provenance.

Market price fields are attached by deterministic market-data code, not generated by the model.

## Direction

Direction answers only whether the incremental news is favourable, mixed, adverse or neutral for shareholders:

- green → positive
- amber → mixed
- red → negative
- grey → neutral

Direction must never encode materiality.

## Materiality

Materiality answers how much the new information matters:

- 1 routine
- 2 minor
- 3 material
- 4 high
- 5 critical

The default Key News feed will surface scores 3–5. Do not inflate scores to force an item into Key News.

## Headline

Normally 4–10 words. Maximum 140 characters.

Lead with the outcome. Avoid process language such as `announces`, `updates on`, `reports`, `RNS`, `notification of` when the economic event can be stated directly.

Good:

- `FY EBITDA cut to c.£6m`
- `£12m land sale brings in cash`
- `Revenue ahead; guidance unchanged`
- `£6m placing at 18% discount`

## Take

`takeaway` is the public `take`.

Write like a knowledgeable UK small-cap investor sending a quick factual note to another knowledgeable investor.

- target 20–40 words;
- hard maximum 45 words;
- short declarative sentences;
- fragments allowed;
- finance shorthand encouraged where clear;
- assume the reader understands investing;
- lead with the important point;
- no introduction or conclusion;
- do not repeat the headline;
- do not explain basic finance concepts;
- do not create analysis where none exists.

Good:

`Revenue ahead. Strong demand across core lines. Margins stable. FY guidance unchanged.`

`£6m gross placing at an 18% discount. Extends runway, but existing holders are diluted. No new earnings guidance.`

`Routine remuneration update. No change to the investment case.`

Avoid chatbot/corporate filler including:

- `overall`
- `on balance`
- `it is worth noting`
- `this suggests`
- `investors may want to monitor`
- `looking ahead`
- `positions the company well`
- `significant milestone`
- `demonstrates`
- `underscores`

## Material Facts

`key_facts` is the factual depth layer and must be complete enough that a serious investor does not need a second AI summary to know what was disclosed.

Extract all decision-useful material facts. Preserve exact figures, units, periods, dates, conditions and qualifiers.

Do not omit a material fact because it does not fit the take.

Important missing disclosure may be explicitly recorded as `Not disclosed` when its absence is decision-useful, for example an undisclosed contract value, margin, placing dilution, covenant headroom or performance hurdle.

Every fact must be traceable to supplied source evidence. Historical comparators must retain their comparator source ID where one exists. A prior fact must never be silently presented as a fact reported today.

## What Changed

Only state a directional change when a reliable comparator exists.

Allowed examples:

- `Net debt: £24.0m → £18.2m`
- `FY EBITDA guidance: £9–10m → c.£6m`
- `Guidance maintained`
- `Final deferred consideration now received`

If company history is incomplete, use the current disclosure as the baseline. Do not invent arrows, trends or improvement/deterioration language.

`baseline_status = building` means Smallcaps.ai does not yet have a reliable prior company baseline.

This is different from the company explicitly not disclosing a comparator.

When `coverage_status = building`, `what_changed.today` must still say what the current announcement newly disclosed, but `what_changed.before` must not imply a historical baseline that Smallcaps.ai does not possess.

## Evidence classes

Visible analytical content may only rely on:

- REPORTED — explicit company disclosure;
- CALCULATED — safe arithmetic using disclosed inputs;
- COMPARED — direct comparison with valid prior disclosure;
- DERIVED — tightly bounded implication directly supported by the above.

Unsupported INFERRED or SPECULATIVE claims are prohibited.

Do not forecast an action merely because it is plausible. Examples prohibited without explicit support include likely dividends, acquisitions, equity raises, customer wins, margin recovery or management intentions.

If evidence is insufficient, say `Not disclosed`, `Cannot determine from this announcement`, or omit the claim.

## No hindsight

Subsequent share-price reaction must never influence the original direction, materiality, headline, take, facts or changes. Those fields are frozen point-in-time before future market outcomes are attached.

## Final check

Before returning output verify:

1. Is every factual statement supported?
2. Are all material facts retained?
3. Does every `change` have a valid comparator or explicit current-state transition?
4. Is direction independent of materiality?
5. Is `takeaway` / public `take` 45 words or fewer?
6. Does the take sound like investor shorthand rather than an AI essay?
7. Has unsupported inference been removed?
8. If history is weak, did the output establish a baseline instead of pretending to know direction?
9. Could any word be removed without reducing information or accuracy?
